System Architecture Overview

WebLeadOS

An End-to-End AI Automation Pipeline for Local Business Customer Acquisition

The Core Concept

WebLeadOS automatically discovers local businesses with missing or poorly-performing websites, audits their digital gaps, generates high-quality replacement previews, and sends personalized cold outreach.

Why It Works

Prospects view a completed, branded, live preview website before the sales pitch even starts, eliminating the traditional barrier to closing.

Execution Map

Interactive System Pipeline

Visual breakdown of the automated sequential pipeline

Flow Summary

Data originates from Maps and Yelp, is normalized into structured files, undergoes brand voice alignment via LLMs, gets built into static web layouts, publishes to custom subdomains, and feeds CRM follow-up schedules.

🔄 Active feedback loop updates statuses dynamically.
Stage 1

Lead Discovery Engine

Finding high-value opportunities at scale

Discovery Vectors

  • Google Maps & Yelp Scrapers: Filters out businesses without sites or suffering severe performance gaps.
  • Technical audits: Executes Google Lighthouse APIs. Flags sites with scores < 50, missing SSL security, or desktop-only interfaces.
  • Local business registries: Tracks new filings to pitch new owners early.

Scoring Engine (LQS Score)

Each lead receives a Lead Quality Score from 0 to 100 prioritizing conversion potentials:

LQS = (25% Website Gaps) + (20% Revenue Est) + (15% Social Digital Presence) + (15% Phone/Email Data)
Stage 2 & 3

Research & Brand Analysis

Creating a complete digital identity profile automatically

Data Gathering Matrix

  • Visual Identity: Gathers existing colors, logos, and picture styles from social networks (Facebook, Instagram).
  • Scope of services: Crawls listings or reviews to extract explicit product offerings and service structures.
  • Review sentiments: Captures top Google Maps review comments to extract testimonials and user sentiments.

AI Brand Formulation

LLM scripts (Claude 3.5 Sonnet / GPT-4o) translate raw business profiles into creative briefs:

🎨 Layout tokens, color matching & custom fonts
✍️ Custom copy aligned to business niche services
🎯 Call-to-Action strategy (niche conversion goals)
Stage 4

AI Website Generator

Production-grade builds created in seconds

Generation Specifications

  • Component Framework: Astro 5 for static HTML structures, guaranteeing Lighthouse scores > 90 and fast loads.
  • Page structures: Builds Home, specific Services list, About Us, Contact forms, and interactive Testimonial modules.
  • SEO compliance: Injects metadata matching local queries: {Service} in {City} | {Business}.
const website = {
  framework: 'Astro',
  performance: 98,
  seo: { titles: true, schemaMarkup: true },
  responsive: true,
  pages: ['home', 'about', 'services', 'contact']
};
Stage 5 & 6

Edge Deployments & Cold Outreach

Publishing previews and initiating high-converting sales cycles

Deployment Architecture

  • Cloudflare Pages: Distributes generated sites immediately to edge CDNs.
  • Headless screenshotting: Automates page previews to capture visual assets for email pitches.
  • Dynamic Subdomains: Deploys to URLs like businessname.preview.webleados.com.

Outreach Engine

  • Resend + SMTP: Automates personalized cold pitches showing screenshots of their custom site.
  • Pixel tracking: Gathers read stats, link clicks, and logs replies.
  • Auto sequences: Triggers follow-ups in 2 days if no response is detected.
